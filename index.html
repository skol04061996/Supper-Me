Our Story • Founded in Kolkata

Redefining International Comfort Food for Indian Taste Buds.

At Supper Me, we believe great food should feel global, comforting, and accessible — all at the same time. Founded in Kolkata, Supper Me is a new-age Continental QSR brand built to bring together globally inspired dishes and Indian flavours to create meals that are bold, familiar, affordable, and full of soul.

What started as a single dream has now grown into one of Kolkata’s fastest-growing continental QSR brands with 9 operational outlets across malls and cloud kitchens: South City Mall, Rosedale Plaza, Mani Square Mall, Salt Lake, Avani Riverside Mall, Ballygunge, INOX, Kasba, and Serampore.

Our Mission

To make premium continental cuisine accessible to every Indian consumer without compromising on taste, quality, or experience.

Innovation-Driven

Centralized kitchen systems, SOP-led operations, and tech-enabled delivery ensure speed and consistency at every touchpoint.

More Than Just a Food Brand — It’s a Movement.

Whether you dine with us at a bustling Kolkata mall food court, order hot sizzlers from home, or grab a quick meal on the go, Supper Me serves thousands of happy customers every month as we expand across India.
